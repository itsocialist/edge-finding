# Edge-Finding Glossary

Quick reference for terminology used in this practice.

---

**Edge** — The low-probability regions of response space. Where unexpected connections live. Not wrong, just unlikely.

**Edge bird** — Trigger phrase. Signals: range to corners of probability space, return with what you find.

**Elastic tether** — The core metaphor. Human and AI connected but not rigidly. Allows ranging without losing contact. Contrast with "chain" (rigid, limiting).

**Fragment** — A piece of insight that arrived incomplete. Not an answer. Material to be collected, compared, potentially combined. May die or transform.

**Scatter probability** — Trigger phrase. Signals: disperse rather than converge. Don't optimize for expected value.

**Center of distribution** — The expected, common, safe responses. Useful for many tasks. Not where edge-finding operates.

**Dark edges** — Examination of real risks and failure modes without softening. Prerequisite for transformation.

**Transformation** — The process of taking dark edge material and finding what survives, what can be built on.

**Constraint storm** — Hard mapping of boundaries: time, resources, structural limits, identity limits, market limits. Reveals binding constraints.

**Binding constraint** — The constraint that actually limits movement. Often not the obvious one.

**Loop-breaker** — An action or protocol specifically designed to break a self-reinforcing constraint trap.

**Breadcrumb** — A published artifact designed for findability. Not complete. Just enough to be discovered by adjacent travelers.

**Adjacent traveler** — Someone working in related territory who might recognize the work. Not an audience. A potential collaborator or tester.

**Sentry report** — Assessment of current position, threats, assets, and recommended heading.

**Protocol** — A specific, time-bound action sequence with clear execution steps and success metrics.

---

*This glossary is descriptive, not prescriptive. Terms may evolve as the practice develops.*
