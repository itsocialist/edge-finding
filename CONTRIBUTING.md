# Contributing to Edge-Finding

This isn't a typical open source project. There's no codebase to fix. The contribution is **exploration data**.

---

## What We Need

### Session Reports

If you try edge-finding with an AI, report what happened:

1. **The question you started with** — what were you actually trying to explore?
2. **What fragments emerged** — what unexpected material came back?
3. **What worked** — which aspects of the practice produced useful exploration?
4. **What didn't** — where did it fall flat, feel forced, or produce noise?
5. **What surprised you** — anything that couldn't have been predicted?

### Variations

Did you modify the practice? Different trigger language? Different phase structure? Report the variation and what it produced.

### Boundary Conditions

Where does edge-finding fail? What kinds of questions does it not help with? Negative results are valuable data.

---

## How to Report

**Option 1: Open an issue**

Use the "Session Report" template. Include as much or as little as feels right. Anonymous reports are fine.

**Option 2: Fork and add**

Add your session to `/examples/` as a markdown file. Submit a PR.

**Option 3: Direct contact**

If you'd rather not post publicly, reach out directly. Contact info in repo description.

---

## What We're Not Looking For

- Polished theory or frameworks (we have enough theory)
- Debates about whether AI can "really" explore (not useful)
- Comparisons to other methodologies (interesting but not the focus right now)
- Promotion or marketing (this isn't that kind of project)

---

## Attribution

All contributions will be credited unless you request anonymity. By contributing, you agree that your session reports can be used to develop the practice further.

---

## The Goal

Edge-finding is documented here as a breadcrumb. We're trying to find out:

1. Does this transfer? Can others use it?
2. What conditions make it work?
3. What variations emerge from different practitioners?

Your reports answer these questions. That's the contribution.
